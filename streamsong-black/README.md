# Streamsong Black – 18-Hole Safe-Play Guide

Team Europe golf day, Fri 20 Nov 2026. Mobile-first course guide for a 16–18 handicap.

- `index.html` – interactive guide: pick a hole, tee (Green/Black/Silver/Gold) and typical drive; the map plots safe targets and shot yardages. Open in any browser.
- `Streamsong_Black_18_Hole_Guide_Phone.pdf` – phone-sized, one hole per page (Black tee, 210-yd drive).
- `Streamsong_Black_18_Hole_Guide.pdf` – A4 print version.

Hole 1 is traced from the Par The Course layout; holes 2–18 are schematic maps from the field-guide notes (yardages from the scorecard, hazard positions indicative). Today's flags, wind and caddie advice take priority.
